OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

RAMANA INFINITY is a developing humanitarian initiative created to honour Ramana's memory and carry forward a spirit of kindness, generosity, and compassion.

Our long-term aspiration is to support children, older adults, vulnerable families, and communities facing hardship through responsible humanitarian programmes and meaningful partnerships.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, care, and the opportunity to live with hope. Our journey begins with a commitment to learn, plan responsibly, and build trust.

"Charity never faileth." 1 Corinthians 13:8 — King James Version

A PERSONAL MESSAGE

A Message from the Founder

Ramana was not my brother by blood, but he was my brother by bond — a bond built on love, kindness, and a connection that will forever remain close to my heart.

RAMANA INFINITY is my way of honouring him and carrying forward his spirit of compassion. I hope to build an initiative that brings people together to support children, older adults, vulnerable families, and anyone facing hardship.

I am still a student, learning and building my future. Although I do not yet have the resources to make a large difference on my own, I believe that a meaningful journey can begin with a purpose, honesty, and a willing heart.

For my brother Ramana, whose bond with me went beyond blood, and for every life that deserves a little more hope.

With love and purpose,
Sachina Sharon R
Founder — RAMANA INFINITY

OUR PRINCIPLES

Transparency and Accountability

Trust is essential to meaningful humanitarian work. As RAMANA INFINITY develops, our aim is to establish clear and responsible practices before undertaking projects or handling funds.

Our intended commitments

  • Verify community needs before announcing a campaign.
  • Establish realistic project plans and budgets before seeking support.
  • Work with appropriate and verified partners and service providers.
  • Maintain accurate records of any future funds received and spent.
  • Publish truthful updates about confirmed activities and outcomes.
  • Respect the privacy, dignity, and safety of the people we hope to serve.
  • Establish appropriate legal and financial arrangements before collecting donations.

These are intended standards for the initiative's development, not a claim that an established charity audit, registration, or reporting system is already in place.
